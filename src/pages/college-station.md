Most people choosing lighting have never done it before — and in College Station, a lot of those people are building a new home with an allowance and a deadline. That's the job we love: take a set of plans, walk it room by room, and turn a pile of decisions into a lighting plan with every fixture, finish and the number at the bottom.

Our showroom floor has about a hundred fixtures and fans from more than seventy-five designer lines, including Visual Comfort, Hubbardton Forge, Arteriors, Schonbek, Hinkley and Hudson Valley — brands you'd otherwise drive to Houston to see. Everything else we can get.

And we're still here after. If a fixture arrives damaged or something quits in two years, you call the showroom on Old Wellborn Road and we handle it with the manufacturer.
