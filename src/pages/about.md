## How this started

A friend from church approached Jeff about buying his lighting showroom. George McCoy heard about it, got excited, and wanted in. Becca was the natural person to lead the new store — she's run it ever since, and built it into what it is today.

That's it. It's not a business story, it's a College Station story: somebody you know from church has a business, a friend gets excited, and it turns out your wife is the one who can run it.

Aggieland Lighting and Design is owned by two local families — Becca and Jeff Keefauver, and George and Amie McCoy.
