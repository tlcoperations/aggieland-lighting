## What we collect

- **Information you give us.** If you call, visit, or send a consultation request, we use what you share (such as your name, phone, email and project details) to respond to you.
- **Website usage.** Like most websites, we use analytics and advertising tools that set cookies or similar technology to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our ads. These may include Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel. They collect information such as pages viewed, links clicked, device and browser type, and approximate location.

## How we use it

To answer your questions and requests, to improve the website, and to show and measure ads for our showroom on Google, Facebook and Instagram. We don't sell your personal information.

## Online store

Our online catalog at shop.aggielandlighting.com is operated with LightsAmerica and has its own privacy policy, which applies to purchases made there.

## Your choices

- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Google: manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
- Meta: manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings.
- To ask about or delete information you've sent us, call {phone}.
