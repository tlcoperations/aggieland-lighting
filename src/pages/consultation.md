## What happens in a consultation

You sit down with one of our lighting consultants and work through your home one room at a time. Layering — how ambient, task, and accent light work together in a real room. Scale, which is the single most common thing people get wrong. Color temperature, and why the same bulb reads warm in one room and clinical in another. How many fixtures, and how big. What to dim and how.

You leave with a lighting plan: every room, every fixture, quantities and finishes, and a number at the bottom.

**It's free.** There's no deposit, no design fee, and no obligation to buy anything from us at the end of it.

## Who it's for

Building a new home in Bryan–College Station and holding an allowance you're not sure how to spend. Remodeling a kitchen, a primary bath, or the whole house. Working with an interior designer and wanting the lighting handled properly alongside them. Or replacing one thing and wanting it to be right.

## What to bring

- Your plans, if you have them — printed or a PDF on your phone
- Your allowance number
- Ceiling heights, if you know them
- Photos of finishes you've already picked: cabinets, tile, countertops, plumbing, flooring
- Anything you've saved that you liked. Screenshots count. So does "I don't know, but not that."

If you have none of that, come anyway. We'd rather start early with half the information than late with all of it.

## We do lighting. We don't do interiors.

If you're working with an interior designer, we work alongside them — not around them. We'll take direction from your designer, spec to their vision, and hand everything back in a form they can use. And if you'd be better served by a full interior designer, we'll tell you and point you to one of the good ones in town.
