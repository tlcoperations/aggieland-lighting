## Brazos County

**College Station · Bryan · Wellborn · Kurten · Millican**

This is home. Our showroom is in south College Station, and there are houses all over Bryan–College Station with our lighting in them. If you're building in one of the new neighborhoods around town — Castlegate, Greens Prairie, Williams Creek, Pebble Creek, The Traditions — bring your plans and your allowance and we'll walk the whole house with you.

Remodeling instead? Bring a photo and the ceiling height. A kitchen, a bath or one fixture over the dining table is often a single afternoon's decision.

</section>

<section class="county" id="grimes-county">

## Grimes County

**Navasota · Anderson · Plantersville · Iola · Bedias · Todd Mission**

A straight shot up Highway 6 from Navasota. Grimes County customers often come in for ranch and country homes — porch and patio lighting, outdoor-rated fixtures, and ceiling fans sized for big rooms and covered porches.

</section>

<section class="county" id="madison-county">

## Madison County

**Madisonville · Midway · North Zulch**

Head west on Highway 21 from Madisonville. For a new home or a big remodel, the trip pays for itself: you'll see fixtures in person instead of guessing from a screen, and leave with a room-by-room plan.

</section>

<section class="county" id="burleson-county">

## Burleson County

**Caldwell · Somerville · Snook · Lyons**

Take Highway 21 east from Caldwell. Lake homes and country homes around Somerville and Caldwell come to us for outdoor lighting rated for the weather, porch fans, and statement fixtures for open living rooms.

</section>
